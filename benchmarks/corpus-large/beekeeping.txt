A healthy honeybee colony keeps one queen, up to about sixty thousand workers in summer, and a few hundred drones. Inspect hives every one to two weeks in warm months, checking for eggs, capped brood, and stored food. Manage mite levels with integrated methods, because parasitic mites can collapse colonies. Leave sufficient honey for winter: roughly eight to ten kilograms for a temperate climate. Understand swarm prevention before the spring surge.
