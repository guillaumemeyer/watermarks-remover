Saddle stitching suits small booklets, while coptic binding lays pages flat for journals. Fold signatures of eight or sixteen pages in multiples of four, since folded sheets must overlap. A bone folder creases paper without damaging fibers. Use a stitching cradle and a waxed linen thread for durability. Clamp the spine before gluing, then add end papers and cover boards, with drying time of at least two hours.
