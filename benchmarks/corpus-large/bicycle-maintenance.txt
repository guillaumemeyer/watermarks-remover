Regular bicycle maintenance keeps a commuter bike reliable. Check tire pressure weekly; most road tires want eighty to one hundred ten psi, while hybrids run fifty to seventy. Clean and lubricate the chain every two hundred miles, wiping off old lube first. Brake pads wear past the wear line in roughly three to six thousand miles and should be replaced before that. Tighten quick-release levers and check wheel trueness after any pothole hit.
