Consistent sleep timing reinforces the body's internal clock. Adults generally need seven to nine hours nightly. Keep the bedroom cool, quiet, and dark; blackout curtains and earplugs help. Avoid screen use within the hour before bed, since blue light delays melatonin release. Caffeine has a half-life of roughly five hours, so afternoon coffee can still reduce sleep at night. A short walk outdoors in the morning anchors the cycle.
